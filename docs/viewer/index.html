Elevator dispatch, simulated

In a modern building you tell the lift where you're going before you board, and a controller decides which car you get. The rule it uses decides how long everyone waits. Try the rules on realistic traffic — or add people yourself.

How it works Full results Source
1Choose trafficA morning rush, lunch hour, a 51-floor tower — or your own CSV. Every rule gets exactly the same passengers.
2Choose a ruleSmart dispatch estimates everyone's journey and picks the car that adds least delay. Nearest car and Take turns are the simple alternatives.
3Watch and compareAmber dots are people waiting. The numbers show typical and worst waits. Compare two rules side by side on identical traffic.
